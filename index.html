As a supernatural being of the elf race, you once possessed dazzling wings. Regrettably, outmatched in combat, you were shattered by the dark supernatural "Nine Heavens God Luo" and divided into seven fragments, each entrusted to different beasts for control.

You must continuously defeat enemies to earn skill points, enhance your talents, and gather and upgrade equipment to grow stronger, so you can reclaim the glory of the Elven race—the Wings of the Elves—from the beastmen and the Nine Heavens Divine Order.

In addition to the spirit form, four other character forms have been unlocked. You can switch your character form in real time to adapt to different terrains and enemies.

In the floating island map, you can use an automated cruising aircraft for transportation and shoot down energy balls in the sky to earn coins, equipment, and more. Experience real-time transformation and aerial combat, while keeping an eye on your mana, health, and soul values. You can exchange coins for these at the spaceship store. With over 40 types of monsters, including ground melee fighters, archers, mages, birds, beasts, insects, and more, it's all waiting for you to challenge.

Gameplay: You can engage in combat with enemies using swords, polearms, whips, and a variety of spells. Additionally, you can freely summon werewolves, mages, dragon knights, and elven spirits to assist you in battling formidable foes.
